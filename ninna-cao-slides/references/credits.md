# Credits and provenance

**Ninna Cao Slides** records Ninna Cao's presentation preferences for meaningful photographs, explanatory diagrams, useful context, automatic motion and closely matched speaking notes. Each brief defines the visual direction. The dark editorial preset records one earlier design direction.

The fixed 1920 × 1080 browser stage and zero-dependency HTML foundation derive from **Frontend Slides by Zara Zhang**:

- Source: https://github.com/zarazhangrui/frontend-slides
- License: MIT, copyright 2025 Zara Zhang.
- The unchanged viewport base is bundled at `assets/viewport-base.css`.
- The full upstream notice is retained at `assets/licenses/frontend-slides-MIT.txt`.

The Ninna layer adds brief-led design, content and evidence guidance, a portable builder, a controller, a notes helper and neutral examples. Each generated HTML includes the MIT notice for the shared viewport base.

Fonts are bundled under their own SIL Open Font License notices:

- Instrument Serif, Instrument Serif Project Authors: `assets/fonts/InstrumentSerif-OFL.txt`.
- Source Sans 3, Adobe: `assets/fonts/SourceSans3-OFL.txt`.

The fonts are unmodified. Generated presentations can embed them. Retain their font license notices when redistributing the font files themselves.

The decorative SVG examples are neutral illustrations prepared for this skill. They are not documentary photos or research evidence. The package does not bundle private interview media, unpublished product designs, commercial research claims or credentials.

The sample Double Diamond is redrawn from the Design Council's framework, available at https://www.designcouncil.org.uk/resources/the-double-diamond/ under CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . The colors, labels and diagram layout have been adapted for this skill's illustrative sample.
