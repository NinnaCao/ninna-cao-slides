# Credits and provenance

**Ninna Cao Slides** records Ninna Cao's presentation preferences: a meaningful visual on every slide, photographs that fade into the stage, explanatory diagrams, useful context, automatic motion and closely matched speaking notes. Each brief defines the visual direction. Cinematic Night records one design direction Ninna approved in November 2026.

## Foundation

The fixed 1920 × 1080 browser stage and zero-dependency HTML foundation derive from **Frontend Slides by Zara Zhang**:

- Source: https://github.com/zarazhangrui/frontend-slides
- License: MIT, copyright 2025 Zara Zhang. The full notice is in `references/frontend-slides/LICENSE`; the original distribution is in `assets/source/Frontend_Slides_Original.zip`.

## Fonts

Bundled in `assets/fonts` as Latin subsets under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, from the Google Fonts repository (https://github.com/google/fonts):

- Barlow Condensed (SemiBold, Bold, ExtraBold), The Barlow Project Authors: `BarlowCondensed-OFL.txt`.
- Manrope (variable), The Manrope Project Authors: `Manrope-OFL.txt`.
- Courier Prime (Regular, Bold), The Courier Prime Project Authors: `CourierPrime-OFL.txt`.

The fonts are subset to Latin characters and converted to WOFF2; otherwise unmodified. Generated presentations embed them. Keep the license notices when redistributing the font files themselves.
