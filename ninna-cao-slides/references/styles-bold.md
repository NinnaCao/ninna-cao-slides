# 34 expressive design recipes

Treat each recipe as a starting point. Use the given character, composition and contrast, and then adapt the colors, typography and assets to the brief.

## arcade-board

**Arcade Board.** Pixel-era demonstrations and short creative talks.

- Palette. Surface `#171331`, primary type `#fcf7ff`, accent `#f3c749`, supporting field `#3f386e`.
- Typography and graphic device. Mono headline, readable sans-serif body and a pixels motif. Readability and writing system determine the final type choice.
- Opening. Frame the title with stepped pixels and a single real product visual.
- Evidence and comparison. Use chunky marks around short numeric cues and keep explanatory type simple.
- Sections and closing. Carry the center hierarchy and signature motif into the divider and takeaway. Keep the focal visual secondary to precise data on dense slides.
- Motion. Use short, decisive entrances with a still reading state.
