# 12 adaptable presets

Treat each recipe as a starting point. Use the given character, composition and contrast, and then adapt the colors, typography and assets to the brief.

## stage-contrast

**Stage Contrast.** A decisive opening for live launches and short pitches.

- Palette. Surface `#17192c`, primary type `#f8f7ef`, accent `#f26243`, supporting field `#502e41`.
- Typography and graphic device. Condensed headline, readable sans-serif body and a band motif. Readability and writing system determine the final type choice.
- Opening. Put the key phrase beside one large, cropped image.
- Evidence and comparison. Pair a comparison with a numbered graphic. Keep captions in a uniform baseline.
- Sections and closing. Carry the split hierarchy and signature motif into the divider and takeaway. Keep the focal visual secondary to precise data on dense slides.
- Motion. Use short, decisive entrances with a still reading state.
